Plan Approval Procedure (Inland Vessels)
Following procedures should be followed for approving plans for all types of inland vessels. The procedure ensure compliance with all the mandatory requirements specified by various inland vessels rules made under the ISO’76.
General Instructions:
Following procedures should be followed for approving plans for all types of inland vessels. The procedure ensures compliance will all the mandatory requirements specified by various The procedure ensures compliance will all the mandatory requirements specified by various
1. All plans submitted must show sufficient details to demonstrate compliance with the relevant rules.
2. Structural Fire Safety-S.R.O. No. 301/Law, dt: 31.10.01, rues-22-35 regarding heat resistant lining, Exhaust piping, Sight glass, Pump room, Steer cage, Air intake/Exhaust ducting is to be followed and described clearly in the submission of drawing for approval.
3. Six sets of plan is to be submitted, including one soft copy in compact disc (CD), where feasible.
4. A list of documents and plans & an index shall be included with each submission;
5. 1:100 Scale to be used in all design & plan, except smaller plans which can be drawn using scale 1:50. In one plan no more than one scale can be used, all dimensions must be in mm.
6. Every plan is to be signed and stamped by the designer, person responsible to check the design, CEO of the design firm and the owner, with date.
7. All designs & plans and documents to be submitted with sequential number,
8. Section modulus calculation for applicable structures such as kilson, floor plate scantling, kilsons & webs, shell & deck scantling, side frame, etc must be submitted along with the drawings.
9. Stability calculation as per Stability rules for inland ship (S.R.O. No. 287/Law, dt:30.10.02) is to be submitted as follows: The underwater shape and KM calculation done- -either manually, or by using computer software. If manually, details of the calculation to be shown separately. If using computer software- software type approval certificate to be submitted to the DOS, that has been approved by any IACS (International Associate of Classification Societies) member society. Requirements of calculation: - Ships up to 70 metre, offset table interval max. 1 cm, based upon the offset table, under water shape and KM calculation to be prepared. - Calculation sheet should calculation of under water volume, water plan area at different draught, calculation of all hydrostatic curve, section modulus. Note: The Stability criteria for inland vessel need to be established or yet to be established, Till that time vessel’s actual stability criteria, calculation manually or by using computer software, to be shown in the proposed plan and need not to be complied with the criteria set in the rule book ( IMO criteria)
10. Demonstration or Compliance with the crowing requirement: With all passengers (full capacity & 75 kg/passenger) on one extreme side on top deck vessel’s heeling must not exceed 10 degree.
11. General Characteristics for main and auxiliary engine and equipments to be submitted and Placing and ventilation of accumulation to be shown.
